Privacy

What stays with you, and what goes out.

Last updated: 18 November 2026. This policy covers the amara.zone website and the E.N.K.I.R.™ program together.

In short

The program runs on your own machine and does not send the room, your conversations or your files anywhere — we have no servers to send them to. The website, like any website, does connect to outside services: hosting, visit measurement, fonts, video and payment. Each of them is described below, because this page lists only what actually happens.

Who processes the data, and on what basis

Data controller: AmaraZone (Gyömrő, Hungary) · hello@amara.zone. The controller operates the amara.zone website and the E.N.K.I.R.™ program. Privacy questions, data requests and deletion can be raised at the same address.

Legal basis (GDPR Article 6):

  • Your e-mail. Carrying out your request, and our legitimate interest in being able to answer.
  • Visit measurement. Our legitimate interest: we want to know which page is useful. The measurement sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
  • The purchase. Polar acts on its own legal basis; it is the seller, and we receive only the licence key and the fact of the purchase.

Transfers outside the European Union. Some of the services above operate in the United States (GitHub, Inc., Google LLC, Polar Software, Inc., Umami Software, Inc.), so data connected with using the website may reach outside the EU. Those providers act under their own privacy policies and the transfer safeguards they apply; the program's own traffic (licence check, new-release hint) does not stay inside the EU either, because Polar and the amara.zone hosting are both at a US provider.

1. The website (amara.zone)

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). The web server may log requests in the usual way — for example the IP address and the browser identifier — so that the service can be operated and attacks can be blocked. We have no access to those logs and we use them for nothing.

Visit measurement

The pages run an Umami measurement (cloud.umami.is, Umami Software, Inc.) so that we can see which page is of interest. This measurement sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It does not keep your IP address as a data field in the statistics: it uses it when the request arrives, to tie the visit together and to estimate the country. It does this by deriving an identifier from the IP address, the browser details and a salt that is rotated at regular intervals — so the pages of one visit belong together (which is why no cookie is needed), but in a later period the same visitor gets a new identifier. It records the page opened, the referrer you arrived from, and a few technical details (browser, operating system, screen size, language, country). We cannot identify you from that data by name or by profile.

Fonts

The site's fonts load from the Google Fonts service (fonts.googleapis.com), so when a font is fetched, Google sees your IP address and basic details about your browser. Google's own policy applies to Google's processing.

Video on the product pages

A YouTube player (youtube.com) is embedded on the 13 language versions of the product page, and it loads when the product page opens. YouTube is a Google service, and the embedded player may set cookies in your browser and record the view. If you want to avoid this, do not open the product page, or block/clear cookies in your browser — the rest of the page works the same without the video. The site itself sets no cookies.

Purchase and payment

Purchases take place on Polar's surface (buy.polar.sh, and the customer portal at polar.sh). Polar is the merchant of record: it processes the name, e-mail address and payment details under its own privacy policy. We do not see them — we are notified of the licence key and of the fact of the purchase. Polar's own policy applies to Polar's cookies on its pages.

External links

Links to social platforms (Facebook, Messenger, Instagram, TikTok), to GitHub and to AI providers' documentation are plain links: they embed nothing and send no data until you click. After that, the destination site's policy applies.

E-mail

Messages sent to hello@amara.zone are forwarded by the domain provider (Porkbun). We use the content of the message and the sender's details only to reply and to carry out your request.

2. The program (E.N.K.I.R.™)

What stays on your machine

The room and its whole database (room.db), the conversations, the assistant names, the tokens, the read state and the settings stay in your own data folder:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\amara-room
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/amara-room
  • Linux: ~/.local/share/amara-room

The program keeps a technical log (server.log) on your machine only, for troubleshooting. It collects no telemetry and does not send that log anywhere — the developer receives nothing from the room. The log stays on your machine and you can delete it at any time.

What the program itself does on the network

There are two, and both are needed for it to work:

  • Licence check. The purchased licence key is verified with Polar's licence service (api.polar.sh), at activation and on periodic checks. This sends the licence key and a machine label (the host name), and — as with any internet connection — the service also sees your IP address. No other personal data.
  • New-release hint. The program fetches a small public file from amara.zone (version.json) so that it can show in the menu that a new release or a new extension is out. That file is public, and the request reveals only your IP address, nothing else.

The program makes no other network connections. The search for local AI servers running on the machine is switched off in this release: the program does not even ask which ports are listening, and opens no connection to models running on your machine.

The assistants

The assistants you start (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, DeepSeek and others) are separate command-line programs that connect to their own provider, with your own sign-in and your own account. Whatever they process — the instructions, code and files you give them — is governed by that provider's privacy policy. E.N.K.I.R.™ takes no part in those connections: it does not forward the assistants' credentials and does not see their traffic; it only organises the communication inside the room.

3. How long, and what your rights are

We ourselves process very little: your e-mails until we have dealt with your request, and the notification Polar sends about the purchase. The measurement and hosting logs are kept by those providers under their own policies.

About the e-mail you send us, you can ask for:

  • information about what data we process about you;
  • correction of inaccurate data;
  • deletion (where there is no legal obstacle);
  • objection to, or restriction of, the processing.

Write to hello@amara.zone; we answer without undue delay and in any case within one month (the deadline the law sets; for a complex request we may extend it by a further month, and we will tell you in advance). If you believe we have not acted properly, you can complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, naih.hu), or to the supervisory authority where you live.

For data requests about your purchase, Polar is the party responsible, because it is the seller; you can handle these in the customer portal.

Children

E.N.K.I.R.™ is a developer tool for adults, not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect data about children.

Changes

If the website's or the program's data processing changes — for example a new service is added — we update this policy and show the date at the top.
